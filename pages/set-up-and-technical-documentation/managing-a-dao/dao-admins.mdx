# Organization admins

> _The user who adds a_ organization _to Tally will automatically be granted Admin privileges. If you have an older Governor_ organization_, it may not have an Admin — reach out to us to start the process of adding one!_

## Admins Section

Admins for a given organization are displayed at the bottom of the [Broken link](/broken/pages/LyBlfgdn940XFb1jagAQ "mention"):

<figure><img src="/images/image-3-2.png" alt="" /><figcaption><p>Admins section of the organization page</p></figcaption></figure>

## Admin Abilities

* Adjust organization settings, including logo, description, and links.
* [Link Gnosis Safes](../using-governor-with-gnosis-safe/gnosis-safe).

## Superadmin Abilities

* All [Admin abilities](dao-admins#admin-abilities).
* Appoint additional Admins and Superadmins.

## Add an Admin

No Admin in your organization? Reach out to the [Tally team](https://tally.xyz/contact).
